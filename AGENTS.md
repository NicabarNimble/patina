- WIT contracts live in `wit/toys/` and child composition lives in `wit/worlds/`.
- SDK tiers are `sdk/patina-sdk-core`, `sdk/patina-sdk-data`, and `sdk/patina-sdk-agent` (with `sdk/patina-sdk` as umbrella re-export).
