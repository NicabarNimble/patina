**Key decisions:**
- Source schema (`wit/schema/github/`) was significantly stale vs installed (`.patina/schemas/github/`). Synced source to match installed, not the other way around — installed version had been evolved correctly over sessions.
- `--with-issues` dropped entirely rather than rewired to `mother run` — [[unix-philosophy]] says flags shouldn't invoke other commands.
- PR `since` uses early-stop rather than client-side filtering — since results are sorted by `updated_at desc`, once we hit an older item, all remaining are older.
- Linked issues extraction uses simple keyword parsing, not regex — avoids adding a dependency for one function.

**Compression pass:** No duplication or unnecessary abstractions introduced. All changes are deletions or targeted fixes.
