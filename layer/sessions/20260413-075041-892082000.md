## Decisions
- **Lean into component-model + wac-graph, not parallel system.** Confirmed via `[[wasi-is-foundation-not-option]]` and `[[compose-over-build]]` — Patina builds *with* WASI ecosystem, custom `patina:*` only for the delta.
- **MCT seams are WIT interfaces.** Inside vs outside toy is a composition decision, not a child-side declaration. Mother arranges, child doesn't know.
- **6 glue adapter children to retire.** `adapters/*-pando` replicate what wac-graph does natively. Adapter retirement plan is the next slice.
- **Canonical component lane is default.** `TypedComponentInvocationDriver` default; `HandleBridgeInvocationDriver` kept for legacy/compat via env var. Mother runtime stays contract-agnostic via dynamic export lookup.
- **Agents are guests, Mother is infrastructure.** Re-grounded in `[[agents-are-guests-mother-is-infrastructure]]` — rivet/agent-os/workerd are potential hosts or deployment targets, not replacements for Mother's belief/federation role.
