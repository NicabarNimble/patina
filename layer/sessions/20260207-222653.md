### Patterns Observed
- **Score-based inside, rank-based outside** is the correct fusion
  architecture. Inner RRF (rank-of-rank) destroyed magnitude information
  twice. Min-max preserves within-table magnitude for the inner merge,
  outer RRF handles cross-oracle combination.
- **Lexical-only P@5 as diagnostic** — comparing lexical-only vs unified
  P@5 isolated the regression to outer fusion, not inner normalization.
  Without this comparison we might have blamed the normalization.
- **P@5 ceiling is an outer fusion problem** — unified P@5 (26%) trails
  lexical-only P@5 (30%) by 4pp. Non-lexical oracles dilute top-5.
  Fixing this requires fusion-layer changes (out of Phase 2.5 scope).
