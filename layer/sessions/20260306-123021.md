### 14:30 - One Protocol, Multiple Transports
Consulted Netflix/Kafka pattern + five audit lenses. Unanimous: one protocol, multiple transports. Key insight: `host_emit` IS already pipe protocol — it emits typed, schema-validated facts over WASM host calls. The rewrite formalizes this and adds native transport (stdio/HTTP). Not a revolution — naming what exists and extending it. Crate structure: `patina-pipe-types` (shared), `patina-sdk` (WASM), `patina-pipe` (native).
