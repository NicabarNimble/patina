## Decisions

- PVR closed at boundary-wording scope. Identifier renames (`plugin_name` → `child_name`) deferred — happen organically, not spec-governed.
- `plugins/doctor` is orphan code (not in execution path). Safe to delete. Doctor WASM unification is a future spec.
- The belief system is the core of Patina. Core verbs serve it. Mother/children/toys extend it.
- Children are data movers with bounded agency, not general-purpose WASM compute.
- 22 toys collapse to 10: 4 WASI + 1 `patina:connect` bridge + 5 Patina-specific.
- Adopt actual `wasi:http` and `wasi:filesystem` directly. Use `patina:log`/`patina:state` as temporary shims until WASI standards stabilize.
- `patina:connect` is the credential-security bridge. Opaque resource handles. Credentials never cross WASM wall.
- Default-deny raw http. Connect-mediated access is the secure default.
- Vocabulary locked to 5 terms: toy, toybox, kind, child, Mother.
- Anti-sprawl rule: no phase changes more than one of {interface contract, runtime dispatch, child business logic}.
- `[[wit-contract-single-source]]` abandoned — absorbed by toy collapse.
- `[[greenfield-crate-extraction]]` blocked on `[[toy-collapse-wasi-alignment]]`.
