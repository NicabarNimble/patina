## Previous Session Context
Added `patina eval --scry-raw` diagnostic to test raw E5-base-v2 (768-dim) vs projected (256-dim) retrieval. Results were conclusive: raw E5 achieves P@10=52.5% vs projection's 9.2% — the trained 2-layer MLP projection was destroying E5's pre-trained structure. The projection should be deleted, not improved. Next step: remove projection for knowledge/sessions domains in `oxidize` and `SemanticOracle`, using raw 768-dim E5 embeddings directly.

- **Mother**: convergence engine (what keeps showing up across projects and people)
