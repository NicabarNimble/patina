# Pando Is Composed Children

A composed group of children working together as one organism is a pando. Latin: pandō — "I spread." A pando spreads the patina.

## Statement

A composed group of children working together as one organism is a pando. Like the Pando aspen colony — 47,000 trees that are actually one organism connected by shared roots through Mother — a pando is many children that appear as one capability to the user. Each child has one job. The pando accomplishes what no single child can.

## Evidence

- [[session-20260331-224232-852361000]] - Vocabulary search through team, crew, family, colony, bloom, fuse, strand — landed on pando via siphonophore → clonal organism → Pando aspen. The metaphor is self-documenting: any LLM resolves "pando" to the Utah aspen colony and immediately understands shared roots, one organism, many parts. (weight: 0.95)
- [[child-construction-canon]] - Three MVPs prove the pattern: folder-text-to-parquet pando (6 children, linear pipeline), multiproject-belief-share pando (federation), e2ee-multimother-chat pando (peer-to-peer). Same children, different pandos. (weight: 1.0)
